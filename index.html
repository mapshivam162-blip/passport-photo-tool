Common Questions

Meri photo server par upload hoti hai?

Nahi. 100% kaam aapke phone ke browser me hota hai. Internet band ho tab bhi chalega (ek baar site khulne ke baad).

Kaunsa size form ke liye sahi hai?

SSC GD: 20–50KB, 413×531px. Railway RRB: 20KB ke aas-paas. UPSC: 40KB ke aas-paas. Neeche editor me "Popular Presets" diye hain — wahan se ek tap karein.

Background kaise badlega?

White / Blue / Gray option plain background (deewar) wali photo par best kaam karta hai. Complex background par result perfect nahi aayega.

HEIC photo kaam karegi?

Browser HEIC decode nahi kar paate. iPhone ki photo ko pehle "Files" app se JPG me convert karein, ya screenshot lein.

Photo ko drag karke face ko frame ke beech me rakhein. Pinch / scroll se zoom.
